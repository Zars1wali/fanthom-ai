The repository [`ZeroPointIntel/sales-ops`](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/README.md) is not a generic chatbot or support ticketing script. It is an **autonomous, deterministic conversational commerce platform** (named **SalesOps / Nuncio**, evolved from the earlier Rabta AI PoC by Fromtribe OÜ and ZeroPointIntel). 

Its sole architectural purpose is to act as an **automated inbound sales rep** across WhatsApp and Web that guides prospective buyers through a complete commercial transaction without human intervention.

Here is how the architecture functions as a complete sales engine:

---

### 1. Deterministic Sales Funnel State Machine
Rather than letting an LLM freely improvise conversations, sales progression is controlled server-side via [FunnelStateMachine](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/funnel/machine.ts#L5-L43) across strict [LEGAL_STAGE_TRANSITIONS](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/funnel/transitions.ts#L3-L13):

$$\text{greet} \longrightarrow \text{discover} \longrightarrow \text{qualify} \longrightarrow \text{present} \longrightarrow \text{objection} \longrightarrow \text{close} \longrightarrow \text{won / handoff / lost}$$

- **State Gating**: An LLM can propose a transition, but the server validates whether the transition is legal (e.g., an agent cannot jump straight to `won` without going through `close`).
- **Event-Driven Triggers**: System events (such as generating a checkout link or capturing an explicit lead) automatically progress the customer to `close` or `qualify`.

---

### 2. Zero-Hallucination Catalog & Quoting Engine
In e-commerce and B2B sales, hallucinating prices, features, or fake discounts creates legal liability. In [`sales-ops`](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/architecture.md#L18-L32), **the catalog is never fed directly into the system prompt**:
- **Server-Resolved Tools**: The agent retrieves data strictly via tools:
  - [`search_catalog`](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/agent/tools/search_catalog.ts) executes PostgreSQL full-text and trigram searches over the active snapshot.
  - [`quote`](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/agent/tools/quote.ts) and [`calculate_vertical_quote`](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/agent/tools/calculate_vertical_quote.ts) calculate totals server-side in integer minor units (`priceMinor`).
  - [`get_policy`](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/agent/tools/get_policy.ts) returns structured warranty, shipping, and return terms.
- **Strict Price Hedging**: If catalog data is older than `maxAgeMinutes`, the tool flags `stale: true`, forcing the agent to hedge prices ("that was €49 when last checked; the site will confirm at checkout") rather than asserting stale numbers.
- **Empty Result Discipline**: If an item is absent, the agent is forced to state non-availability and offer human handoff instead of guessing.

---

### 3. Sales-Trained Conversational Psychology & Agent Policy
The policy generated in [renderSystemPolicy](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/agent/policy.ts#L14-L100) encodes strict sales heuristics derived from real-world merchant conversion audits:
- **Direct Price First**: If a customer asks *"How much is this?"*, the engine provides a one-line price answer immediately. It forbids stalling with discovery questions that cause drop-off.
- **Single Diagnostic Questioning**: When a customer is undecided, the agent asks **one** high-value question (e.g., *"What do you sell, and where do your customers message you today?"*) instead of an interrogation questionnaire.
- **Customer State Profiling**: Evaluates whether the shopper is `curious`, `ready_to_buy`, `comparing`, `price_sensitive`, `skeptical`, or `hesitant`.
- **Anti-Manipulation & Transparency**: Strictly prohibits manufactured urgency (e.g., fake countdowns or false scarcity) and includes mandatory **EU AI Act Article 50** badges stating that the visitor is conversing with an AI agent.

### 5. Multi-Channel Commercial Pipeline
The runtime bridges omnichannel customer traffic into a unified sales database:
- **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API**: Handles inbound WhatsApp queries, normalizes voice notes with Deepgram Nova-3 transcription ([deepgram.ts](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/channels/deepgram.ts)), and chunks responses into natural 280-character bursts with 1200ms delays ([chunker.ts](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/packages/core/src/channels/chunker.ts)).
- **Web Widget**: Next.js App Router widget with SSE streaming and responsive drawer/floating modes.
- **Catalog Ingestion Adapters**: Pulls products directly from [catalog-woocommerce](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/adapters/catalog-woocommerce), [catalog-shopify](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/adapters/catalog-shopify), [catalog-csv](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/adapters/catalog-csv), or [catalog-json](file:///C:/Users/waliz/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/sales-ops/adapters/catalog-json).
- **Merchant Control Plane**: Business owners can monitor sessions and intervene using commands (`/pause`, `/resume`, `/takeover`, `/status`).

### Summary
`ZeroPointIntel/sales-ops` is a sales engine because it **ingests a merchant's inventory**, **ingests customer intent across chat channels**, **evaluates qualification through a formal sales funnel**, **quotes prices without hallucinations**, and **executes checkout, appointment booking, or lead capture** as an autonomous commercial agent.
